# M5.2 grouped bar series

M5.2 adds semantic grouped vertical bars with category centers, group offsets,
values, width, baseline, face/edge style, series order, legend ownership, and
deterministic rendering. Stacked, horizontal, malformed, and arbitrary-group
variants retain precise diagnostics.

Tests use small synthetic category matrices and cover positive, mixed-sign,
multi-panel, profile, figure-set, JSON, lifecycle, and LuaLaTeX paths.
